# CAD
3D printed parts (.3mf), printed in PLA on a Bambu A1 mini.

- `turret_base.3mf` - holds the pan servo upright, shaft over the center, with screw posts behind it for the buck converter, ESP32, and MOSFET module
- `pan_bracket.3mf` - sits on the pan servo horn and holds the tilt servo on its side
- `tilt_head.3mf` - mounts to the tilt servo horn, holds the blower by its outlet and the thermal camera right above it
- `all_parts.3mf` - Bambu Studio project with all three parts ready to print (draft 1 on two plates, draft 2 on one)

## Draft 1
First full print, set up as a fast, light test print. The cross shaped horn recess didn't come out on the pan bracket and tilt head, so the horns sit flat and are held by the 4 horn screws.

## Draft 2
Fixes from assembling draft 1: horn recess fixed and made deeper and looser, pockets for the horn screw heads so the tilt servo and fan sit flush, a full-height pocket with end lips that the tilt servo slides into, a bigger and thicker fan sleeve, M2 posts for all the boards, and a camera tab with screw holes and nut pockets. The base is narrower with the boards packed tighter and posts for the two lever splitters (12V and 6V), so the whole turret fits on one A1 mini plate. Printed at normal strength (0.20 mm Standard).
