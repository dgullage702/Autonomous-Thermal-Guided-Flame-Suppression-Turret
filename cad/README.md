# CAD
3D printed parts (.3mf), printed in PLA on a Bambu A1 mini.

- `turret_base.3mf` - holds the pan servo upright, shaft over the center, with screw posts behind it for the buck converter, ESP32, and MOSFET module
- `pan_bracket.3mf` - sits on the pan servo horn and holds the tilt servo on its side
- `tilt_head.3mf` - mounts to the tilt servo horn, holds the blower by its outlet and the thermal camera right above it
- `turret_v1_all_parts.3mf` - Bambu Studio project with all three parts on two A1 mini plates (base on one, head and bracket on the other), set up for a fast test print
